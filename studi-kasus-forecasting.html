Masalah bisnisBusiness problem

Target yang ditebakA target that was guessed

Dulu target GMV bulanan ditentukan dari insting musiman. Tim tahu polanya: saat musim masuk sekolah belanja bergeser ke kebutuhan sekolah dan penjualan sepeda turun, lalu bulan berikutnya pulih. Dari situ target dinaikkan sekian persen, tanpa dihitung dari GMV periode sebelumnya sebagai dasar.

Monthly GMV targets used to be set from seasonal intuition. The team knew the pattern: in the back-to-school season spending shifts to school needs and bike sales drop, then recover the next month. The target was then bumped up by some percent, without being computed from the prior period's GMV as a basis.

Arahnya sering benar, tapi angkanya menebak. Dan target yang menebak susah dipertanggungjawabkan saat ditanya kenapa segitu.

The direction was often right, but the number was a guess. A guessed target is hard to defend the moment someone asks where the number came from.

Keputusan yang dijawabThe decision it answers

Siapa yang pakai angka iniWho uses this number

Target GMV jadi patokan untuk tiap tim toko: berapa yang harus dikejar bulan ini. Dari satu angka itu manajemen otomatis menimbang dua hal berikutnya: stoknya cukup atau tidak untuk mengejar target, dan campaign marketplace mana yang harus diikuti agar tercapai.

The GMV target becomes the benchmark for each store team: how much to chase this month. From that single number, management weighs two follow-on questions: is there enough stock to reach it, and which marketplace campaigns to join to get there.

Jadi satu angka target menyentuh keputusan stok dan keputusan promosi sekaligus, bukan sekadar isi laporan.

So one target number touches both the stock decision and the promotion decision, not just a line in a report.

Karena pembacanya berbeda, sistemnya punya dua tampilan: Owner untuk ringkasan keputusan, dan Analis untuk metodologi lengkap, supaya tiap orang mendapat bentuk yang dia butuhkan.

Because the readers differ, the system has two views: Owner for the decision summary, and Analyst for the full methodology, so each person gets the form they need.

PendekatanApproach

Angka yang bisa diaduA number that can be contested

Untuk tiap marketplace, 22 model kandidat diadu, dari baseline paling sederhana sampai keluarga Holt dan Theta. Pemenang dipilih dari MAPE terkecil lintas seluruh fold backtest, sehingga yang menang adalah yang akurat secara konsisten, dan tetap harus lolos sanity gate sebelum dipakai. Kalau model sederhana seperti moving average justru paling akurat, itu yang dipakai. Model yang rumit belum tentu menang.

For each marketplace, 22 candidate models are run, from the simplest baseline to the Holt and Theta families. The winner is chosen by the smallest MAPE across all backtest folds, which rewards consistent accuracy, and it still has to pass a sanity gate before being used. If a simple model like moving average is the most accurate, that is what gets used. A complex model does not necessarily win.

Kenapa MAPE, bukan RMSEWhy MAPE, not RMSE

MAPE dipilih karena berbentuk persen, jadi kebaca oleh manajemen yang non-teknis dan bisa dibandingkan antar toko yang skalanya jauh berbeda. RMSE menempel ke angka absolut: error 200 juta pada GMV 5 miliar tidak informatif saat GMV-nya 3 miliar.

MAPE is chosen because it is a percentage, so non-technical management can read it and it is comparable across stores of very different sizes. RMSE sticks to absolute figures: a 200-million error on 5-billion GMV is not informative when GMV is 3 billion.

Hasil dan buktiResult and evidence

Cara membaca akurasinyaHow to read the accuracy

Pada satu bulan contoh, target yang ditetapkan sekitar Rp 4,6 M dan realisasinya sekitar Rp 4,5 M, dengan MAPE sekitar 9%. Selisih setipis itu memberi titik acuan bahwa angkanya sejalan dengan keadaan data dan kapasitas stok.

In one example month, the target set was about Rp 4.6 B and the actual came in around Rp 4.5 B, with MAPE around 9%. A gap that small shows the number was in line with the data and the stock on hand.

Tidak semua kanal sama. Shopee biasanya di bawah 10%. TikTok hampir selalu di atas 10% dan bisa lewat 15%, karena penjualannya digerakkan konten (video, live, affiliate), bukan niat beli langsung seperti marketplace murni. Untuk kanal yang liar, kadang GMV tiga marketplace digabung agar deretnya lebih stabil, bukan dengan mengubah angka, tapi lewat agregasi.

Not every channel is equal. Shopee is usually below 10%. TikTok is almost always above 10% and can exceed 15%, because its sales are content-driven (video, live, affiliate), not direct purchase intent like a pure marketplace. For a volatile channel, the three marketplaces' GMV is sometimes combined to stabilise the series, not by changing numbers but through aggregation.

DampakImpact

Dari angka ke tindakanFrom number to action

Pada satu bulan, minggu pertama GMV jatuh jauh di bawah laju harian (dipantau dari GMV sebulan dibagi jumlah hari). Penyebabnya dicek langsung, ternyata stok produk kunci kosong. Rekomendasinya: percepat kedatangan stok lewat jalur manajemen, agar perolehan akhir bulan tidak jauh dari target.

In one month, the first week's GMV fell well below the daily run rate (monitored as monthly GMV divided by the number of days). The cause was checked directly: a key product was out of stock. The recommendation: speed up the restock through management, so the month-end result would not drift far from target.

Sejak metode ini terbukti, manajemen bergeser dari menebak ke memakai forecasting untuk menetapkan target bulan depan, dengan kalibrasi manusia: bila stok bulan depan diperkirakan tipis, target diturunkan sesuai kondisi lapangan. Sistemnya jadi titik awal keputusan, bukan kotak hitam yang dipercaya buta.

Since the method proved itself, management shifted from guessing to using the forecast to set next month's target, with human calibration: if next month's stock looks thin, the target is lowered to match field conditions. The system is a starting point for the decision, not a black box trusted blindly.

KeterbatasanLimitations

Kapan angkanya tidak boleh dipercayaWhen the number should not be trusted

Batas yang diakui terbukaLimits stated openly

  • Kalau riwayat datanya terlalu pendek, forecasting tidak dipaksakan. Lebih aman memakai pendekatan growth dulu sampai periodenya cukup panjang untuk diuji.
  • If the data history is too short, forecasting is not forced. It is safer to use a growth approach first until the period is long enough to be tested.
  • Faktor musiman besar seperti masa sekolah, puasa, dan Lebaran berada di luar kendali model dan harus dibaca bersama konteksnya.
  • Large seasonal factors such as the school season, the fasting month, and Lebaran sit outside the model's control and must be read alongside their context.
  • Ketersediaan stok, campaign dadakan, dan belanja iklan tidak menjadi variabel penjelas. Forecast membaca hasil penjualan, bukan penyebabnya.
  • Stock availability, sudden campaigns, and ad spend are not explanatory variables. The forecast reads the sales outcome, not its cause.
  • Forecasting yang tiba-tiba terlihat sempurna justru dicurigai. Dalam analisis data, kesalahan selalu ada, dan angka yang mulus biasanya berarti datanya memang mudah, bukan modelnya hebat.
  • A forecast that suddenly looks perfect is treated with suspicion. In data analysis, error is always present, and a smooth number usually means the data was easy, not that the model was brilliant.
