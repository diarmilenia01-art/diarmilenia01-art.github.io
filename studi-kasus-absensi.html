STUDI KASUSCASE STUDY

Dari gajian telat ke laporan sehari yang tetap manusiawiFrom late payroll to a one-day report that stays humane

Bagaimana perhitungan keterlambatan, potongan, dan lembur yang bikin gajian molor berubah jadi laporan sehari, dengan aturan yang seragam tapi tetap menyisakan ruang keputusan manusia.How lateness, deduction, and overtime calculation that delayed payroll becomes a one-day report, with uniform rules that still leave room for human judgment.

Angka pada demo publik bersifat ilustratif, bukan data perusahaan. Yang ditunjukkan di sini adalah cara berpikir dan sistemnya. Figures in the public demo are illustrative, not company data. What is shown here is the thinking and the system.
Masalah bisnisBusiness problem

Gajian yang telat karena hitung manualPayroll late because of manual counting

Perhitungan keterlambatan, potongan, dan lembur sepenuhnya manual. Absen ditarik tanggal 26, laporannya baru diterima sekitar tanggal 29, dan hampir selalu ada revisi. Akibatnya gajian tertunda dan karyawan yang belum menerima gaji mulai mengeluh.

Lateness, deduction, and overtime calculation was fully manual. Attendance was pulled on the 26th, the report arrived around the 29th, and there was almost always a revision. Payroll slipped, and staff who had not been paid began to complain.

Rumus sudah ada, tapi tidak dipakai optimal karena HR menilai ada banyak pertimbangan manusia sebelum angkanya bisa dipakai apa adanya.

Formulas existed but were not used optimally, because HR judged there were many human considerations before the numbers could be used as-is.

PendekatanApproach

Aturan ditulis sekali, revisi di tempatRules written once, revised in place

Dengan dashboard ini, data absensi mentah langsung diunggah dan prosesnya tinggal klik, tanpa input ulang satu per satu. Aturan ditulis sekali lalu diterapkan seragam: jadwal sif, toleransi telat, potongan, uang makan, dan tarif lembur. Rekap lembur cukup dengan memilih siapa yang lembur, lalu terkumpul sendiri untuk keuangan. Laporan selesai dalam sehari, dan revisi bisa dilakukan langsung di sana.

With this dashboard, raw attendance is uploaded directly and the process is a few clicks, without re-entering anything. Rules are written once then applied uniformly: shift schedules, lateness tolerance, deductions, meal allowance, and overtime rates. The overtime recap just needs picking who worked overtime, then it compiles itself for finance. The report is done in a day, and revisions happen right there.

Ada dua keluaran: PDF yang rapi dan rinci namun sulit diedit, dan Excel yang angkanya bisa diubah.

There are two outputs: a clean, detailed PDF that is hard to edit, and an Excel whose numbers can be changed.

Yang membedakanWhat sets it apart

Sistem yang manusiawi, bukan potong butaA humane system, not blind deduction

Alasan wajib ditulisA reason is mandatory

Sebuah rumus mentah akan memotong siapa pun yang terlambat. Padahal keterlambatan karena insiden, misalnya ban bocor, tidak adil kalau langsung dipotong. Di dashboard ini ada mekanisme usulan dan keputusan: ditentukan apakah suatu kasus dipotong atau tidak, dan alasannya wajib ditulis. Tanpa alasan, kasus itu tidak bisa diproses.

A raw formula would deduct anyone who is late. But lateness from an incident, say a flat tire, is unfair to deduct outright. This dashboard has a proposal-and-decision mechanism: it is decided whether a case is deducted or not, and the reason must be written. Without a reason, the case cannot be processed.

Hasilnya adalah jejak yang bisa diperiksa, dan keputusan tetap di tangan manusia untuk hal yang memang butuh pertimbangan.

The result is an auditable trail, and the decision stays with a human for what genuinely needs judgment.

KeterbatasanLimitations

Efektif, bukan sempurnaEffective, not perfect

Sistem ini tidak mengklaim bekerja 100% otomatis, dan memang tidak dirancang begitu. Kasus yang butuh pertimbangan tetap diputuskan manusia, dan di situlah nilainya. Yang bisa dirasakan nyata adalah efektivitasnya: laporan yang dulu molor berhari-hari kini selesai dalam sehari. Nama dan gaji pada demo ini seluruhnya dummy.

The system does not claim to run 100% automatically, and was not designed to. Cases needing judgment are still decided by a human, and that is where its value lies. What is tangibly felt is its effectiveness: a report that used to drag for days now finishes in one. Names and salaries in this demo are entirely dummy.
