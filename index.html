I build the data systems a company runs on day to day: setting sales targets, deciding when stock should be reordered, and keeping prices sensible against their costs.

Setiap sistem dirancang untuk menjawab satu keputusan tertentu: forecast, reorder, klasifikasi, rekonsiliasi, atau harga. Yang saya tentukan bukan hanya hasilnya, tapi cara hasil itu sampai, supaya angkanya tetap berdiri saat dipertanyakan.

Each system is designed to answer one specific decision: forecast, reorder, classification, reconciliation, or pricing. I decide not just the result but how the result is reached, so the numbers still stand when questioned.
